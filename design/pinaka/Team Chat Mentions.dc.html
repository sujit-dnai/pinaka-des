Reply by long-press or the Reply button ("Replying to {name}", quote in bubble). My messages right in navy; others left with an initial avatar. Time formats, the Tamil mic, read ticks, unread badges, scroll-to-bottom, "No messages yet. Start the team conversation!".
AddedType "@" for this case's members only; filter as you type; tap or Enter to insert a chip; Backspace removes the whole chip. Non-members show "Not in this case's team". Sent @names in bold blue open a person card with Call. Mentions of me get a yellow band and "@ you". The push arrives even when muted and opens the chat at the message with a flash. "@" badges on the case card and workspace chat icon, "Mention" type in the bell, and a Mentions screen.
