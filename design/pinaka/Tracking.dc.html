TTracking rebuild · design only
PhoneThe phone tracks while moving and sleeps when still. There is one quiet ongoing notification ("On duty · Moving" / "On duty · Stopped since 10:40 am") that changes only when the state changes. A one-time battery guide covers Xiaomi, Oppo, Vivo, Realme and Samsung. Below 20% the phone sends less often, and below 10% only stops and case events are sent. Removed permission and mock location show as clear banners.
PortalThe Live Board has 6 states. Green shows the stop place and time, grey means no status for 45 min on duty, and red means location off or permission removed. FO 360 shows one officer's day and phone health. Route Tracking draws moving lines and stop dots sized by time, gives the day's km from moving points only, and flags jumps.
Remote Config12 keys with defaults from the brief (4d), changed from the portal without an app update. The Nearest Officer ranking in Allocate takes the current stop point as the officer's position.
