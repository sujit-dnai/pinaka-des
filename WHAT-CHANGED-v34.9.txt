TRANSLATOR ROOT CAUSE: THE STORE APP SENT "{}" INSTEAD OF THE AUDIO (28-Sep) — pinaka.html (app www/index.html)
  · The new "recent clips" log showed every clip from the Android app arriving as 2 bytes: "{}".
    The app's native network layer (CapacitorHttp, Capacitor 8.5) sends a plain Blob as JSON "{}";

  · Fix: binBody() wraps every binary upload as a File in the store app — Translator clips (and the
    offline re-send), photos / selfie (upload), bills (expense/bill) and scanned PDFs. The web app
    (taasenclaims.com/pinaka) is unchanged. Needs the new APK.
